# E-Benta: System Flow for Presentations

## What is E-Benta?

E-Benta is a **marketplace platform for electronic device disposal and repair**. It connects sellers (people with old devices) with buyers (repair shops, recyclers, or refurbishers) to give devices a second life while supporting environmental sustainability.

---

## The Main Problem We Solve

- **Sellers:** How do I safely dispose of old electronics?
- **Buyers:** Where can I find quality devices to repair or refurbish?
- **Result:** E-Benta creates a trusted bridge between both sides

## SELLER JOURNEY: "I Have Old Electronics"

### Step 1: Setup Account
- Register on E-Benta
- Create seller profile
- Submit profile details (admin verification applies to buyers only)

### Step 2: List Devices
- Take photos of devices
- Select device type (Laptop, Phone, Monitor, etc.)
- Add condition details (working/not working)
- Set asking price
- Device appears on marketplace

### Step 3: Receive & Evaluate Offers
- Buyers submit offers (may offer different prices)
- See buyer profile and ratings
- Accept best offer or negotiate

### Step 4: Arrange Pickup
- Confirm pickup location and time
- Buyer arranges transportation
- Receive payment

**Seller Benefits:**
✓ Fast, safe disposal  
✓ Get paid for old devices  
✓ Help environment (devices get reused instead of landfilled)  
✓ Verified, trusted buyers only

---

## BUYER JOURNEY: "I Need Electronics"

### Step 1: Browse Marketplace
- Create buyer account
- Search by device type (Laptop, Phone, etc.)
- Filter by condition, brand, price
- View seller ratings

### Step 2: Submit Offer
- Found a device you want
- Submit offer with your price
- Wait for seller response

### Step 3: Negotiate (if needed)
- Seller accepts, rejects, or counters
- Chat with seller if prices don't match
- **30-minute grace period:** Can cancel offer if you change your mind

### Step 4: Pickup & Payment
- Arrange pickup time/location
- Inspect device before paying
- Complete transaction
- Leave seller a rating

**Buyer Benefits:**
✓ Lower cost than retail  
✓ Variety of brands and models  
✓ Transparent buyer ratings  
✓ Protection: Cancel within 30 minutes if needed  
✓ Direct contact with sellers

## ADMIN ROLE: "We Keep It Safe"

Admins ensure the marketplace is trustworthy:

1. **Verify Buyers** - Check identity, approve new buyers
2. **Monitor Listings** - Remove inappropriate or false listings
3. **Handle Reports** - Investigate buyer/seller disputes
4. **System Health** - Ensure payments process, data integrity, environmental tracking
